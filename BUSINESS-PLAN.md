# Million Rand Business Model - Chat Product

## Executive Summary
Transform chat-product into R1M+ annual recurring revenue (ARR) SaaS for South African SMBs (consultants, lawyers, agencies). **Target: R83k MRR in 12 months**.

**Model**: Freemium → Paid tiers. R99 one-time joining + R199-R999/mo subscriptions.
- **Y1 Goal**: 5k joining (R500k), 500 Pro subs (R600k ARR).
- **LTV**: R6k/user (12mo avg).
- **CAC**: R200 (SEO/affiliates/Google Ads).

## Pricing Tiers (ZAR)
| Tier | Price | Features | Target |
|------|-------|----------|--------|
| Joining Fee | R99 one-time | Platform access, profile | All |
| Starter | R199/mo | Unlimited chat, basic meetings, support | Solopreneurs |
| Pro | R499/mo | +Video, analytics, API, priority support | Agencies |
| Enterprise | R999/mo | +White-label, custom AI, dedicated server | Corps (custom quote) |

**Upsell**: 20% joining → Starter (month 2), 10% Starter → Pro.

## Market & Go-to-Market
**TAM**: 500k SA SMBs needing client comms (R10B market).
**Acquisition**:
1. **SEO/Landing**: "WhatsApp business chat + meetings" → R10k/mo Ads budget.
2. **Affiliates**: 20% rev share for CPA accountants/lawyers.
3. **Partnerships**: Xero/Quickbooks integrations.
4. **Viral**: In-app referrals (R50 credit).
5. **Content**: YouTube tutorials, LinkedIn (R5k/mo).

**Churn Target**: <5%/mo (high-touch onboarding).

## Tech Stack for Scale
- **Payments**: PayFast (SA) + Stripe (int&#39;l).
- **Infra**: AWS ECS + RDS + Redis + S3 + CloudFront/WAF.
- **Scale**: 10k users → Auto-scale, 99.9% uptime.
- **Security**: 2FA, SOC2 stubs, GDPR-compliant.

## Ops & Team
- **Founder-led**: Solo initially.
- **Costs/mo**: R15k (cloud/ads/tools).
- **KPIs**: 20% MoM growth, <3% churn.

## Risks & Mitigations
- **Payments**: Sandbox test 100%.
- **Competition**: Unique SA focus + meetings bundle.
- **Churn**: Weekly NPS, auto-downgrade.
